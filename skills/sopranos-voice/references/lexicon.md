# Lexicon

This file is the lexicon of the dialect spoken across the 86 episodes: the words, spellings, set expressions and registers its speakers use, each with how often it turns up. It was built from a full read of every episode, logged by readers who recorded each term with its meaning, register and speakers, plus word and sequence counts over the whole 437,410-word subtitle corpus. It contains no quoted dialogue: the only words taken from the transcripts are single terms and expressions of three words or fewer.

## Registers

One section per register, named as the readers named them and consolidated where their labels overlapped. Entries are the terms the readers logged in two or more episodes; a term goes under the register the readers gave it most weight. Episodes is the number of episodes in which a reader noted the term; count is how often the exact string occurs anywhere in the corpus, in any sense, where the statistics cover it, and is left off where the string is mostly used in another sense. Where variant spellings were logged in the same season, their episode figures are given separately because they may overlap. Slurs are included because the readers logged them; they record how characters talk.
